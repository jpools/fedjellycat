Say hello to the dear little hopper that started it all – the legend that is Bashful (Beige) Bunny!

She’s a loyal friend and cheerful companion who always looks on the bright side of life. And with her velvety fur and personalised, floppy ears – in a range of fonts and thread colours – she’s the perfect friend for someone truly one of kind.

She’ll be a great listener, and, if you’ve gained her trust, she might even whisper back to you.
